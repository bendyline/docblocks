# Code of Conduct

## Be nice. Don't be a jerk.

Treat other people with respect. Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking
the person behind them, assume good intent, and make room for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

In particular:

- Be constructive, patient, and professional.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate against, or deliberately
  intimidate anyone.
- Do not publish another person's private information or sensitive security
  details.
- Stop behavior that another participant or a maintainer reasonably identifies
  as unwelcome.
- Respect maintainer decisions about project scope, including the policy that
  pull requests may contain proposals under `specs/` but not code changes.

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other interactions
connected to DocBlocks. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock
threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to keep the community safe and productive.

To report a concern, email [support@bendyline.com](mailto:support@bendyline.com).
Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

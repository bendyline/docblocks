# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability or for any report that
contains exploit details, credentials, private data, or other sensitive
information. Email [support@bendyline.com](mailto:support@bendyline.com) with a
subject that identifies the message as a DocBlocks security report.

When possible, include:

- the affected DocBlocks surface, package, and version;
- the potential impact and conditions required to reproduce it;
- clear reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- any known mitigations; and
- a safe way to contact you with follow-up questions.

Please do not include real credentials or private user data. Allow the
maintainers a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address the report
before disclosing it publicly.

General security-hardening suggestions that do not disclose a vulnerability or
sensitive details may be submitted as a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/bendyline/docblocks/issues/new).

Security reports are reviewed on a best-effort basis. The project does not
promise a particular response or remediation timeline and does not currently
operate a bug-bounty program. Fixes are generally prioritized for the latest
release; support for older releases is evaluated case by case.

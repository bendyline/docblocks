# Oregon coast road trip, 8 to 15 August 2026

Car: the green hatchback, 38 mpg. Route: south from Astoria to Brookings along Highway 101, 7 nights. With Mina and Priya for the first half, solo after Newport when they flew back.

## Day by day

### Sat 8 Aug: Astoria
Drove up from home in 5 hours. Climbed the column for the view, then dinner of fish and chips at a dock cafe. Fog all day.

### Sun 9 Aug: Nehalem Bay
Set up the tent at site 41. Nehalem Bay site 41 had hot showers that took quarters, $1.50 for five minutes, and the bath house was a short walk. Wind at night. Biked the spit with Priya, 4 miles each way, saw elk in the dunes at dusk. Fire ban in effect, so we used the propane stove for chili.

### Mon 10 Aug: Cannon Beach
Haystack Rock at low tide, tidepools with starfish and anemones. Low tide was at 9:12 am and we got there at 8:45. Rented a small cabin because rain was forecast. Mina made a pot of tea and read all afternoon.

### Tue 11 Aug: Pacific City
Climbed the big dune, 2 hours up and down with the shoes full of sand. Fresh crab sandwich at a stand by the beach for 14 dollars. The dory fleet launches directly from the beach, we watched three come in.

### Wed 12 Aug: Newport
Aquarium (24 dollars), the lighthouse, and the bayfront sea lions, loud and smelly. Priya and Mina flew home from the small airport next morning, so we said goodbye over chowder.

### Thu 13 Aug: Florence
Sand dunes, sandboarding rental 20 dollars for 2 hours. Tried it twice, fell ten times. Old town and a taffy shop. Hostel room had a shared kitchen, which I used for pasta.

### Fri 14 Aug: Bandon
Face Rock at sunrise, then miles of beach walking. The lighthouse near the river mouth. A cranberry bog tour. Dinner of oysters.

### Sat 15 Aug: Drive home
Came back inland on Highway 38 and Interstate 5, 9 hours with stops. Gas was 4.89 at the coast and 4.35 inland.

## Money

About 1,350 dollars total for the trip; 480 on lodging, 330 on gas and the rest on food and tickets.

## Mistakes and lessons

1. Fires were banned, I had to rely on the stove entirely.
2. Should have booked Cannon Beach 3 months earlier; the cabin was the last one.
3. Take quarters for showers.
4. Check tide tables before planning beach days (use the tide chart in the phone).
5. A 38 mpg car makes a 700 mile trip cheap.

## Wildlife

Elk on the Nehalem spit at dusk, a dozen sea lions in Newport, grey whale spouts off the cape at Depoe Bay on the 12th, two bald eagles near Florence, and a banana slug the size of a hand. Tufted puffins at Haystack: we saw 2 through binoculars.

## Weather

Cool mornings (12 C) and sunny afternoons around 20 C. Fog hung around the north coast for three days; the south coast was warmer and dry. Pack a wool hat even in August.

## Things to do next time

## Driving notes

- Highway 101 is slow: average 38 mph including towns, so budget 6 hours for what the map says is 4
- Fuel stops: Seaside, Lincoln City, Reedsport, Coos Bay; do not let it fall below a quarter tank south of Florence
- Parking at viewpoints is free, but state beach lots near Cannon Beach charge 5 dollars a day; the pass for the whole week is 10 dollars
- Tunnels on the north coast are narrow; bike riders appear without warning
- Cell coverage vanishes between Cape Perpetua and Reedsport. Download offline maps first

## Playlist and car habits

Made a playlist called "Pacific" of 3 hours; ran out on the second day and fell back on podcasts. Kept a bag of clementines and pretzels in the door pocket. Rule: no driving past 6 pm, sunset is the deadline for the tent.

# Packing list (international, one week, carry-on only)

## Documents

- [ ] Passport
- [ ] Printed booking confirmations
- [ ] Travel insurance policy number on the phone
- [ ] Credit cards, 1 spare in a different bag
- [ ] Cash in small bills

## Clothes

- [ ] 4 shirts, 2 trousers, 1 pair of shorts
- [ ] Light rain jacket
- [ ] 6 pairs of socks and underwear
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes (already broken in)
- [ ] 1 nice outfit for dinners

## Tech

- [ ] Phone and charger
- [ ] Type F plug adapter, bring two, plus a small power strip
- [ ] Power bank (under 100 Wh, carry-on only)
- [ ] Headphones
- [ ] Kindle

## Toiletries (100 ml bags only)

- [ ] Toothbrush and paste
- [ ] Deodorant
- [ ] Sunscreen 50 ml
- [ ] Blister plasters
- [ ] Painkillers and a few antihistamines

## Last minute

- [ ] Unplug the toaster
- [ ] Empty the fridge of anything that spoils
- [ ] Water the pots
- [ ] Set the thermostat to 15 C in winter
- [ ] Put the bins out

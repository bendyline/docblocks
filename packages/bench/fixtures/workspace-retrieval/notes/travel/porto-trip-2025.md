# Porto, 12 to 15 May 2025 (add-on after Lisbon)

Took the Alfa Pendular train from Lisbon Santa Apolonia on Monday 12 May, 3 hours, 31 euros each with a seat reservation. Three nights.

## Hotel

Small hotel in Ribeira, €88 a night with breakfast, room on the top floor with a view of the Douro. Reception closes at 11 pm, they give a code. Better value than the Lisbon apartment but louder at night because of the bars.

## What we did

- Tue 13 May: walked across the Dom Luis I bridge on the top deck, took the cable car down (6 euros), port cellar tour in Gaia with tasting of three ports (15 euros each)
- Wed 14 May: the bookshop with the red staircase, which has a ticket fee of 10 euros redeemable against a book, queue 40 minutes. Not worth it unless you love the stairs. Lunch at the market hall
- Wed evening: the river cruise at sunset, the six bridges tour, 18 euros
- Thu 15 May: Serralves gardens in the morning, flight home at 6:20 pm from Porto airport, metro from the centre, 2.60

## Food

Francesinha at Tasca do Miguel was the best one, €11.50 with fries. It is a sandwich of ham, sausage and steak covered in cheese and a spicy beer sauce. Heavy. One between two is enough. Also:

- Bacalhau a bras at a place near Sao Bento station, 9 euros
- Pasteis de nata at a bakery in Cedofeita were not as good as Lisbon's
- Tripe stew, the local specialty, I tried it once

## Comparison with Lisbon

Porto is smaller and flatter in the centre but the riverfront is steep. Cheaper by about 15 percent. Weather was a few degrees cooler and wetter: rain on the afternoon of the 14th. The tiles on the church facades near Sao Bento are better than Lisbon's.

## Notes

- Andante card for the metro, 0.60 euros
- Port wine: bought 2 bottles of a tawny, 20 year, 38 euros each, carried in the checked bag in socks
- Everyone speaks English but appreciates Portuguese

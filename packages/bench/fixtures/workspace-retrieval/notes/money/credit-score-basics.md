# Credit score basics

FICO scores run 300 to 850. Five factors, with approximate weights:

- Payment history: 35%
- Amounts owed (utilization): 30%
- Length of credit history: 15%
- Credit mix: 10%
- New credit and hard inquiries: 10%

Keep utilization under 30% of each card limit; under 10% is better. Pay at least the statement balance each month and set autopay for the minimum as a safety net. A hard inquiry stays on the report two years but only hurts for about one. Older accounts help, so do not close the first card. Free reports weekly at annualcreditreport.com.

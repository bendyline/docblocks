# Knee physio routine (left knee, from 3 Feb 2026)

Therapist: Lena. Diagnosis: patellofemoral pain, not a tear. Eight sessions booked, one a week.

## The home programme

Every other day, about 25 minutes. On off days walk 30 minutes.

1. Heel slides, 2 sets of 15 per side
2. Straight leg raises, 3 sets of 12, hold 2 seconds at the top
3. Terminal knee extensions with the green band: 3 sets of 15, every other day
4. Step-downs from a 6 inch step, slow, 3 sets of 10
5. Clamshells with the light band, 2 sets of 20
6. Wall sits, 4 x 30 seconds, knees at 60 degrees only
7. Calf stretch, 3 x 30 seconds

## Rules

- Pain up to 3 out of 10 is fine, above that stop
- No deep squats or lunges for 6 weeks
- Take stairs one at a time if it is bad
- Ice for 10 minutes after if swollen

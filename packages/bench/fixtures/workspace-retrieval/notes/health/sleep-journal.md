# Sleep journal, autumn 2025

Trying to fix waking at 3:30 am. Tracking with the ring plus a few lines each morning.

**Mon 29 Sep** Bed 11:40, up at 3:30, back asleep 5:10. Two coffees after lunch. Tired all day.

**Fri 3 Oct** Cut off caffeine after noon starting Oct 3, and fell asleep faster within a week. Today's entry: bed 11:00, up once at 4:15 only.

**Tue 7 Oct** Slept through! 7 h 05. Late dinner was light.

**Sat 11 Oct** Wine at dinner and a bad night. Woke at 2:50. Wine goes on the Friday-only list.

**Thu 16 Oct** Phone out of the bedroom, charging in the hall. Read 20 pages of a paperback instead. Asleep by 11:15.

**Mon 20 Oct** Room at 17 C with the window cracked. Best night so far, 7 h 40.

**Wed 29 Oct** Cold snap, added the second blanket. Still waking once.

## Things that seem to help

- Last caffeine by noon
- Screens off at 10:00
- Cool room, 17 to 18 C
- Walking after dinner
- Magnesium glycinate 200 mg at 9 pm (uncertain, but harmless)

## Things that do not

- Napping after 3 pm
- Alcohol at all
- Checking the clock at 3 am

## Targets

7 hours average over 14 days. Bed by 11:00, up at 6:45.

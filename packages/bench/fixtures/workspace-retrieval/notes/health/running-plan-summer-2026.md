# Half marathon plan, summer 2026

Race: Harbor Lights Half, Sunday 21 June 2026, start 7:00 am, 13.1 miles along the waterfront. Goal: finish under 2:15, which is about 10:18 per mile. Stretch goal 2:10. I only got the all-clear from the physio on 24 March, so this is a compressed 12-week build starting Monday 30 March, being careful of the left knee.

## Principles

- Three easy runs, one quality session, one long run per week
- Easy means conversational, about 10:50 to 11:30 per mile
- Add no more than 10 percent mileage per week
- Every fourth week is a cutback
- If the knee hits 4 out of 10 pain, stop and walk home
- Do the physio band work twice a week, even on busy weeks

## Gear

- Shoes: Tempo Glide 4 in the blue, about 190 miles on them; second pair arriving in May
- Belt with two soft flasks (300 ml each)
- Gels: 6 in the cupboard, one every 40 minutes on long runs past 9 miles
- Watch alerts: pace band at 10:00 to 10:30 for the race
- Shirt with a pocket for the phone

## The 12 weeks

Week 1 (30 Mar): long run 5 miles easy, Tuesday 4 x 400 m with 2 min rests. Total 14 miles.

Week 2 (6 Apr): long run 6 miles easy, Tuesday 5 x 400 m. Total 16 miles.

Week 3 (13 Apr): long run 7 miles easy, Tuesday 20 minutes tempo at 9:50. Total 19 miles.

Week 4 (20 Apr, cutback): long run 5 miles easy, Tuesday 4 x 400 m. Total 14 miles.

Week 5 (27 Apr): long run 9 miles at 10:30 per mile, Tuesday 5 x 800 m. Total 22 miles.

Week 6 (4 May): long run 11 miles at 10:05 per mile, Tuesday intervals 6 x 800 m. Total 25 miles.

Week 7 (11 May): long run 12 miles with the last 3 at race pace, Tuesday 25 minutes tempo. Total 26 miles. This is the peak week.

Week 8 (18 May, cutback): long run 8 miles easy, Tuesday 4 x 800 m. Total 19 miles.

Week 9 (25 May): long run 13 miles, easy for 10 and race pace for 3, Tuesday 3 x 1 mile at 9:45. Total 26 miles.

Week 10 (1 Jun): long run 10 miles, Tuesday 6 x 800 m. Total 23 miles.

Week 11 (8 Jun): taper begins. Long run 8 miles, Tuesday 20 minutes tempo, total 18 miles.

Week 12 (15 Jun): race week. Tuesday 3 x 400 m only, short easy runs, Sunday is the race. Total 8 miles plus the race.

Practice fuelling on every run over 8 miles; do not try new things on race day. Last long run before the race ate: toast with peanut butter, 1 banana, and half a cup of coffee. Hydration: 300 ml every 4 miles. Electrolyte tablet in one flask for runs over 10 miles in warm weather.

## Routes

- The waterfront out-and-back from the boathouse: flat, 3.2 miles each way, my default for long runs because there are water fountains at miles 1.5 and 4.6
- The reservoir loop: 5.4 miles, soft gravel, kind to the knee but hilly in the last mile
- The cemetery loop on Wednesdays: 3.1 miles, quiet, one gentle hill I use for strides
- The track at the high school, open after 6 pm: for 400 m and 800 m repeats, lane 1, count laps with the watch beep

## Warm-up before quality days

1. 5 minutes of brisk walking
2. Leg swings, 10 each direction per leg
3. 10 bodyweight squats, shallow only
4. 4 strides of 20 seconds, building to about 9:00 pace
5. Then the main set; finish with 10 minutes of very slow jogging

## Weather rules

- Above 24 C: slow the pace by 20 seconds per mile and carry water
- Below 2 C: wear the thin gloves and a buff
- Thunder in the forecast: move the run to the treadmill at the gym, 1 percent incline
- Bad air day, index over 100: swap the run for a bike ride or a swim

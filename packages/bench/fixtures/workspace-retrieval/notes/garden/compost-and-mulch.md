# Compost and mulch

## The bin

Two-bay pallet bin behind the shed. Bay 1 is the working pile, bay 2 is finishing. Size 3 ft cube each. Started September 2024.

## Greens and browns

Aim for about 2 parts browns to 1 part greens by volume.

- Greens: kitchen scraps, coffee grounds from the cafe on Alder St, grass clippings (thin layers only), spent plants
- Browns: shredded leaves, torn cardboard, straw, wood chips in small amounts
- Never: meat, dairy, cooked food, dog waste, diseased tomato vines

## Routine

Turn the pile every 9 days with the fork, more often in July. A properly hot pile reaches 55 to 65 C in the centre within a week; I check with the long probe thermometer. If it smells of ammonia add leaves. If it is dry and not heating, add water until it feels like a wrung-out sponge.

Finished compost takes about 4 months in summer and 8 in winter. It is ready when it is dark, crumbly and smells like a forest floor.

## Mulch

- Straw for tomatoes and squash, 4 inches deep
- Shredded leaves for the perennial bed, 3 inches
- Wood chips only on the paths, delivered free by the tree crew, last load 11 Oct 2025
- Keep mulch 2 inches away from stems

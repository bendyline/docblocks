# Raised bed layout (2026)

Four beds on the south side of the yard, built from cedar planks in April 2024.

| Bed | Size | 2026 crop | Last year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 4 x 8 ft | tomatoes (Sungold, Brandywine) | beans |
| B | 4 x 8 ft | tomatoes (San Marzano, Black Krim), basil | zucchini |
| C | 4 x 8 ft | garlic, planted 19 October 2025, harvest early July | peppers |
| D | 3 x 6 ft | salad greens, radishes, carrots | tomatoes |

## Rotation rule

Tomatoes follow legumes, never tomatoes. Beans and peas go where the heavy feeders were. Garlic follows a nitrogen-rich crop.

## Soil

Each bed gets 2 inches of compost every spring and a dusting of kelp meal. Soil test in March 2026: pH 6.6, phosphorus fine, potassium a little low.

## Ideas

- Trellis for cucumbers on the north edge of bed D
- Marigolds around bed B
- Drip line on a timer, 20 minutes at 6 am

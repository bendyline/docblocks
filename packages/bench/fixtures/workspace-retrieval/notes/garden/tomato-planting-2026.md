# Tomato planting 2026

Seeds started indoors on 14 March under the shop light in the spare room. Varieties:

- Sungold (cherry, orange), 6 plants
- Brandywine (big, slow, pink), 3 plants
- San Marzano (paste), 4 plants
- Black Krim, 2 plants, experimental

## Timeline

| Date | Task |
| --- | --- |
| 14 Mar | Sow in 72-cell trays, heat mat at 24 C |
| 28 Mar | Pot up into 4 inch cups once true leaves appear |
| 20 Apr | First hardening off, 2 hours of shade a day |
| 2 May | Last frost date here is about 25 April, so safe now |
| 9 May | Transplant into beds A and B |

## Transplanting rules

- Space the plants 24 inches apart in the bed, with stakes driven in first
- Bury the stem up to the first true leaves; the buried part sprouts roots
- Add a handful of crushed eggshell and a tablespoon of bone meal to each hole
- Water in with fish emulsion diluted to half strength
- Tie to the stake with soft strips of old t-shirt every 10 inches of growth
- Mulch with straw once the soil hits 18 C, not before

## Feeding

Every 2 weeks from first flower with a tomato fertilizer (5-10-10). Calcium is important for San Marzano because they get blossom end rot first; the cure is steady watering, not more calcium spray.

## Pruning

Pinch suckers on Brandywine and Black Krim. Let the Sungold run wild, it is a monster. Remove leaves touching the soil.

See the tomato disease note for what to watch for in July.

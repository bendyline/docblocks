# Pest fragments

- Aphids clustered on the kale stems again; blast with the hose, then spray soapy water
- Slugs in the lettuce row: beer traps in yogurt cups, empty every 3 days
- Cabbage white caterpillars: cover the brassicas with the white mesh from late May
- Squash bugs: hunt for the copper-coloured egg clusters under leaves every Sunday
- Ladybugs bought for the roses were a waste, they flew off in an hour

# Cognitive bias quick list (from the econ course)

- Anchoring: first number heard drags later estimates toward it.
- Availability: judging frequency by how easily examples come to mind (plane crashes feel common).
- Confirmation bias: seeking evidence that agrees with what I already think.
- Hyperbolic discounting: preferring $50 now over $60 next week, but $60 in 53 weeks over $50 in 52.
- Status quo bias: defaults win; this is why opt-out retirement enrollment gets participation above 90%.
- Overconfidence: most drivers rate themselves above average.

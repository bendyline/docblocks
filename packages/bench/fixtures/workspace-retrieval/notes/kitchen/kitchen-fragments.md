# Kitchen fragments

- Rice cooker ratio is 1 cup jasmine to 1.1 cups water
- Hard-boiled eggs: 11 minutes from boiling, straight into ice water
- Cast iron: never soak, dry on the burner, rub with a few drops of oil
- Pasta water should taste like a mild sea
- Buy the good butter (cultured, 82 percent) only for biscuits and toast
- Knife guy at the Saturday market sharpens for 6 dollars a blade
- Out of: baking soda, parchment, white pepper

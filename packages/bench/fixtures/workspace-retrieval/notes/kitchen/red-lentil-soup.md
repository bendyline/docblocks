# Red lentil soup with lemon (serves 6)

Our standard Sunday soup. Costs under 8 dollars and freezes well.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups red lentils, rinsed until the water runs clear
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 3 carrots, diced small
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 6 cups vegetable stock
- Juice of 1 lemon, plus zest of half
- 1 1/2 tsp salt, more to taste
- Handful of chopped parsley

## Method

1. Warm the oil in the big pot over medium heat. Cook onion and carrot for 8 minutes until soft.
2. Add garlic, tomato paste and spices; stir for 1 minute until it smells toasty.
3. Pour in the stock and lentils. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer.
4. Simmer 25 minutes until the lentils are soft but not collapsing.
5. Blend roughly a third with the stick blender so it is creamy but still chunky.
6. Stir in lemon juice, zest and parsley. Taste, add salt.

## Notes

- Do not add the lemon early, it turns bitter.
- A spoonful of plain yogurt on top is good.
- Freezes for 3 months in 2 cup tubs. Reheat with a splash of water.
- Mina likes it with a fried egg on top.

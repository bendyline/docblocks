# Sourdough starter (Gertrude)

Started 2 March 2025 from a jar of rye flour and tap water left on the counter. She finally got bubbly on day 9. Kept in a 1 litre glass jar with a loose lid, never sealed.

## Feeding routine

- Feed it every 12 hours at room temperature (about 22 C in the kitchen)
- Ratio is 1:1:1 by weight: 50 g starter, 50 g bread flour, 50 g lukewarm water
- Discard everything else before feeding, or the jar overflows by lunchtime
- Stir until no dry flour is left, scrape the sides, put a rubber band at the new level so I can see the rise
- A ready starter doubles in about 5 hours after a feed and smells like yogurt and green apple

## Fridge mode

When I am not baking that week:

1. Feed once, wait 1 hour
2. Lid on, into the fridge at the back
3. Feed once a week, pulling it out for 2 hours first
4. Two days before baking, take it out and go back to the 12 hour schedule

## Troubleshooting the starter itself

- Grey liquid on top (hooch) just means it is hungry. Pour it off or stir it in, then feed.
- Pink or orange streaks means throw it away and start over. This has never happened.
- If it is sluggish in winter, move the jar to the top of the fridge where the router sits, or use water at 30 C.

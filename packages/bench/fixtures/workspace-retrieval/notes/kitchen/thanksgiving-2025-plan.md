## The turkey

This year I am following the dry-brine method I tried on the practice chicken in October, scaled up. No wet brining; the fridge cannot hold a bucket.

Dry-brine the 14 lb bird for 52 hours, uncovered on the bottom shelf of the fridge. That means salting it on Tuesday around noon. Salt quantity: 1 tablespoon of kosher salt per 4 lb, so 3 1/2 tablespoons total, plus 1 tsp of baking powder rubbed into the skin for crispness and 2 tsp of black pepper. Pat dry first. Put it on a rack over a sheet pan so air gets underneath.

Thursday morning: take it out at 9:30 to lose the chill. Do not rinse. Rub the skin with 3 tablespoons of softened butter mixed with chopped sage and thyme. Stuff the cavity with a halved lemon, an onion and a handful of parsley stems, nothing else.

Oven 425 F for the first 30 minutes, then drop to 325 F. Plan about 13 minutes per pound at that point, which puts a 14 lb bird at roughly 2 3/4 hours. Pull the turkey when the thickest part of the thigh reads 165 F. It rests 40 minutes under foil, which is when the oven is free for stuffing and rolls. In goes the bird at 11:30 am if I want it resting by 3:15 and carved at 4:00.

Probe thermometer lives in the second drawer. Check that the batteries still work. Last year it died at the worst moment.

## Gravy

- Tuesday: roast the neck, gizzard and 2 lb of wings at 425 F for 45 minutes, simmer with onion, carrot, celery and bay for 3 hours, strain. Should give about 2 quarts of stock.
- Thursday: whisk 1/4 cup flour into 1/4 cup turkey fat from the pan, add 4 cups stock, simmer 10 minutes.
- Dad's pot: same, but with unsalted stock and a splash of cider vinegar for brightness.

## Stuffing

Sourdough from the loaf I bake on Saturday, cubed into 3/4 inch pieces, dried in a 250 F oven for 40 minutes. 8 cups of cubes. Leeks (3 large) and celery (4 stalks) cooked in 6 tablespoons butter, sage 2 tbsp chopped, 1 1/2 cups stock, 2 eggs. Bake covered for 25 minutes and uncovered for 20 at 375 F. Do it in the 9 by 13 dish, not inside the bird, so Colm and Wren can eat it.

## Cranberry sauce

12 oz bag of cranberries, 3/4 cup sugar, 3/4 cup orange juice, zest of one orange, a pinch of salt and a cinnamon stick. 12 minutes at a low boil until most berries pop. Cool, remove the cinnamon, refrigerate. Makes about 2 cups and keeps for a week. Wren gets a separate bowl with no zest.

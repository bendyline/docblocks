# Japanese particles and verb forms (N5 grammar)

Textbook chapters 3 to 8 in one place. Japanese is subject-object-verb, and particles come after the word they mark, acting like little grammatical labels.

## Sentence basics

The verb goes at the end. Word order of the rest is flexible because particles say who does what. Subjects and objects are often dropped when obvious from context. Sentences end with です (desu, polite "is") or a verb in -ます form.

Example: 私は毎日学校でにほん語を勉強します. (I study Japanese at school every day.)

## Core particles

- は (pronounced wa) is the topic marker: it flags what the sentence is about, often with a contrast flavor. 私は学生です, "As for me, I'm a student."
- が marks the grammatical subject, often new information or a question word: 誰が来ましたか, "Who came?" Also used with 好き (like), 分かる (understand), and いる/ある (exist).
- を (pronounced o) marks the direct object: パンを食べます.
- に marks a destination, a point in time, or an indirect object: 七時に起きます (I get up at 7), 友達にあげる.
- へ (read e) is direction, similar to に for travel.
- で marks the place of an action or the means: 図書館で勉強する, バスで行く.
- の links nouns like "of": 私の本, and nominalizes in some constructions.
- と means "and" (complete lists) and "with": 友達と話す.
- も means "also", replacing は, が or を.
- から and まで are "from" and "until": 九時から五時まで.
- か at the end turns a statement into a question, no question mark needed.

The confusing pair is は vs が. Rule of thumb: は sets the stage, が picks out the actor. "Where is the library?" answer: 図書館はあそこです.

## Verb groups

Dictionary form to polite form:

| Group | Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 (u-verbs) | final -u becomes -imasu | かく (write) becomes かきます |
| Group 2 (ru-verbs) | drop る, add ます | たべる becomes たべます |
| Irregular | する becomes します; くる becomes きます | |

Tenses (polite): present/future ます, negative ません, past ました, past negative ませんでした. Example: 食べます, 食べません, 食べました, 食べませんでした.

## The te-form

Needed for requests, ongoing actions, and linking verbs. Rules for group 1 verbs depend on the ending:

- く becomes いて (かく to かいて; exception: いく to いって)
- ぐ becomes いで
- む, ぶ, ぬ become んで (のむ to のんで)
- る, う, つ become って (かう to かって)
- す becomes して

Group 2: drop る, add て. する gives して, くる gives きて.

Uses: ください for requests (見てください, "please look"), ている for ongoing action or state (勉強しています), てもいいです for permission.

## Counting and numbers

Numbers 1 to 10: いち、に、さん、し/よん、ご、ろく、しち/なな、はち、きゅう/く、じゅう. Many counters change sounds: ひとつ, ふたつ, みっつ for general objects; 一人 (ひとり), 二人 (ふたり) for people.

# Walden (Thoreau) reading log

Thoreau lived at Walden Pond from July 4, 1845 to September 6, 1847: two years, two months, two days compressed into one literary year. Built a cabin for about $28. Chapter 1, "Economy", is the long one; skip ahead if bored, but the line "the mass of men lead lives of quiet desperation" is here.

Liked best: "Where I Lived, and What I Lived For" ("I went to the woods because I wished to live deliberately"), and "The Ponds". Less liked: the bean-field chapter, a bit precious.

Note to self: reread in the winter, when the pond-freezing chapter fits the season.

# Upstairs bathroom renovation log (2026)

Scope: gut the 5 x 8 ft upstairs bathroom, new tub surround, new floor, new vanity, new fan. Budget for the household (not work) was 11,500 dollars, with a hard ceiling of 13,000. Started 12 January 2026. Target done by 20 February so that Aunt Ruth could stay in March.

## Before

- 1962 pink tile, cracked in two places, grout black in the corners
- Cast iron tub, kept it (too heavy and it was in good shape), refinished in place
- Vinyl floor over plywood, soft near the toilet
- A fan that sounded like a small aircraft and vented into the attic (not outside)
- Single outlet, no GFCI

## Week by week

### Week 1 (12 to 18 Jan)

Demo. Pulled the vanity, toilet and old tile with a pry bar and a hammer. The tile came off the plaster in a single sheet in one spot and took half the wall with it. Found a patch of old water damage behind the toilet: the subfloor had a soft area about 14 inches across. Cut it out and sistered in new 3/4 inch plywood. Dumpster rented for a week, 285 dollars, ran out of room on day 5.

### Week 2 (19 to 25 Jan)

Rough-in. Dario moved the supply stubs and replaced the old gate valve with a quarter-turn. Beatrix ran a new 20 amp circuit and put in a GFCI outlet. New fan vented through the roof instead of into the attic; she did the roof jack in an hour. Inspection passed on the 24th with no comments.

### Week 3 (26 Jan to 1 Feb)

Backer board and waterproofing. Used cement board on the tub wall, 1/2 inch, screwed every 8 inches. Taped the seams with alkali-resistant mesh tape. Two coats of roll-on waterproofing, 24 hours between coats. Flood test on the floor: 24 hours with an inch of water behind a plug. No leak.

### Week 4 (2 to 8 Feb)

Tile. Tub wall done in 2 days, using a 1/16 inch spacer, starting from a level line at the third course, not the tub rim, because the rim is not level (off by 3/8 inch across the length). Floor hex sheets on the 4th. Cuts around the toilet flange took an hour of wet saw cursing.

Cost surprise: second box of subway tile, 62 dollars, because of breakage and an extra row.

### Week 5 (9 to 15 Feb)

Grout and caulk. Grout is Silver Birch, unsanded, 1/8 inch joints on the wall and 1/16 inch on the floor, sealed after 72 hours. Two small bags, 14 dollars each. Caulk at the corners in white, not grout, because corners move. Mixed the grout in small batches because it kicks fast.

Vanity installed on the 12th. Faucet on the 13th. Toilet back on the 14th with a new wax ring and a new supply line, braided steel.

### Week 6 (16 to 22 Feb)

Paint (Sea Salt Green, satin), towel hooks, mirror, shelf. Final clean. Finished on 21 February, a day late. Ruth arrived on the 3rd of March and said it looked like a hotel.

## Money

Total spent: 5,962 dollars in materials and trades per the table, plus the dumpster, a new wet saw blade, a tile cutter rental and 2 hardware store trips of random stuff. Actual all-in about 6,850. Well under budget because I did the demo, tiling and paint myself. A contractor had quoted 11,200.

## Mistakes

1. Did not check the tub was level before starting.
2. Ordered only 10 percent extra tile; should have ordered 15.
3. Forgot to hang the door before painting.
4. Let the grout dry in a draught; two lines hairline cracked and had to be redone.
5. Bought the fan before checking the duct size. 4 inch, fine, but cheap luck.

## Maintenance

- Reseal grout on the floor every 2 years (due Feb 2028)
- Wipe the walls after showers
- Clean the fan grille twice a year
- Check caulk each spring, redo if it separates

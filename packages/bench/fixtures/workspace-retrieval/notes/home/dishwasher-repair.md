# Dishwasher repair: Aquaflo DW-640

Bought 2019. Started leaving a pool of grey water at the bottom in November 2024, fixed in February 2025 after I finally stopped ignoring it.

## What happened

1. Cycle ran, then stopped with a flashing light and a code.
2. E24 on the display means the drain pump cannot empty the tub. The manual (PDF in the downloads folder) agrees.
3. Checked the filter basket first: gross but not the problem.
4. Pulled the unit forward 8 inches, unscrewed the drain hose from the sink disposal inlet.
5. The disposal knockout plug was never removed when the machine was installed. Hmm, no, it had been, but a lump of rice and a bone fragment had packed the hose elbow.

## Fix

- Flushed the hose with the garden hose until clear
- Cleaned the pump cover under the lower spray arm (3 screws, T15 driver)
- Found a cracked glass shard stuck in the impeller; removed with needle-nose pliers
- Reassembled, ran a hot cycle with a cup of white vinegar

Total cost: nothing, about 90 minutes. A plumber quoted 160 dollars.

## Next time

- Run the disposal before starting the dishwasher
- Rinse rice and bones off plates, scrape not soak
- Clean the filter basket monthly (1st Sunday)
- Part number for a new drain pump if ever needed: DP-640-B, about 48 dollars

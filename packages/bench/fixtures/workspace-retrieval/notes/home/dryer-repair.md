# Dryer repair: Brisa BD-7200

Electric dryer, bought 2020. Died in a way that looked like the end on 4 September 2026.

## Timeline

- Late August: loads taking two cycles to dry, assumed it was the lint trap
- 4 Sep: drum spun fine but the air stayed cold; the thermal fuse had blown, replaced with part 4390-77
- 5 Sep: bought the fuse online, 11 dollars plus shipping, arrived 9 Sep

## What I did

1. Unplugged it (obviously), pulled it away from the wall.
2. Cleaned the whole vent run with the brush kit. A pile of lint the size of a loaf came out. This is probably why the fuse blew.
3. Removed the back panel, 8 screws, 5/16 nut driver.
4. Found the fuse on the blower housing, the small white plastic part with two spade terminals.
5. Tested it with the multimeter: no continuity, so definitely blown.
6. Swapped in the new one, then also tested the heating element: 9.8 ohms, fine.
7. Put it all back, ran a 20 minute test cycle with a towel.

## Result

Heats again. Exhaust temperature at the vent hood measured 60 C.

## Lessons

- Clean the vent duct once a year, in September, not just the lint screen
- Do not replace the fuse without finding the cause (vent blockage)
- Keep a spare fuse in the parts drawer, bought two
- The outside flap sticks; added a bit of silicone spray

Different appliance from the dishwasher, different symptoms, different part.

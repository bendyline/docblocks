# Call: Ridgeline Ski Patrol, September 18 2025

On the call: Marcus E, me, Gwen T (operations director at Ridgeline), Bo L (their lift maintenance lead).

Ridgeline runs 14 Pika-3 stations across their mountain, feeding wind and temperature into their morning avalanche-control briefing. They were happy with accuracy but unhappy about two things.

## Complaints
1. Dashboard data sometimes lags during storms. Gwen said that on a heavy day in February the numbers were almost an hour behind, which matters because patrol decides road and lift openings from them.
2. The wind sensor on the ridge-top station reads low in rime ice conditions. Bo suspects the cup anemometer is icing up.

## Asks
- Alert if any station stops reporting for more than 30 minutes
- A heated anemometer option for the ridge-top station
- Export of one winter's data in CSV for their insurer

## Our answers
We promised the offline alert in the next dashboard release. The heated anemometer is not in the catalog; Marcus will check with Hana whether a retrofit kit is realistic. CSV export is available today from the dashboard under Reports.

## Commercial
Contract renews December 1 at $31,500 a year. Gwen said they would renew if the lag problem is addressed. They want a check-in in the spring.

# On keeping a notebook

## Why I still write by hand

I have kept a paper notebook for most of my working life. Writing by hand is slow, and that slowness is the point. It gives a thought time to settle before the next one arrives.

## What gets lost

A notebook cannot be searched, and I have lost more than one good idea in an old volume. I accept the loss. Not everything I think deserves to be found again.

## A small habit

Most evenings I read back the day's page. It takes a minute. It is the part of the habit I would keep if I had to give up the rest.

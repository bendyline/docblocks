# How the company runs

## The chain of command

The company is a strict hierarchy. The chief executive sets the direction at the top. Three vice presidents turn that direction into plans. Twelve team leads run the daily work. At the base, ninety engineers build the product.

## Why projects slip

Three factors explain most delays. Requirements keep changing after work has started. Teams have too few senior engineers. Funding is often released two months late.

## How a release ships

First, the release manager drafts the notes. Then the build is tagged and tested overnight. Next, it rolls out to a small group of customers. Finally, it goes to everyone.

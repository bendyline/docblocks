# Northstar second quarter review

## The quarter in numbers

Active teams reached 18,420 this quarter. Weekly retention rose to 71%. Expansion revenue came in at $3.8 million. Activation improved to 49% after guided setup shipped.

## From visitor to customer

Each month 48,000 people visit the site. Of those, 6,200 start a trial. Only 1,900 finish setup, and 640 become paying customers.

## Three choices for next quarter

The team has three choices. It can fund a redesign of permissions setup. It can protect the activation gains from guided setup. It can defer advanced mobile editing until next year.

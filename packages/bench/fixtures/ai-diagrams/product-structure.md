# Inside the greenhouse kit

## What is in the box

The greenhouse kit consists of three main parts: the hub, the sensors, and the app. The hub includes a radio, a battery pack, and a small display. The sensors come in three types: temperature probes, humidity probes, and soil moisture probes. The app is made up of a dashboard, an alerts page, and a settings page.

## Warranty

Every kit carries a five-year warranty that covers parts and labour.

# On writing well

## Clarity first

Good writing respects the reader's time. It says one thing at a time, chooses plain words over impressive ones, and trusts the reader to keep up. Most drafts improve when a third of their words are removed, because what remains finally has room to breathe.

## Voice

A distinctive voice is not decoration. It comes from caring about the subject and from the courage to sound like yourself rather than like everyone else in the room.

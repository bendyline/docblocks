# The Harlan Pour-Over Kettle

## Product Overview

The Harlan Pour-Over Kettle is an electric kettle designed for making pour-over coffee. It has a gooseneck spout and adjustable temperature control. It is available in black and in white.

## Features

The kettle has a gooseneck spout. The spout allows the user to pour water slowly and accurately. This is important for pour-over coffee because the water should be poured in a steady stream over the grounds.

The kettle has temperature control. The user can set the temperature from 40 °C to 100 °C in one-degree steps. The kettle will hold the selected temperature for up to 60 minutes. This is useful for making tea as well as coffee, because different teas need different water temperatures.

The kettle has a capacity of 0.9 liters. This is enough water for about four cups of coffee.

The handle is made of walnut. It stays cool when the kettle is hot. The lid has a small knob that is also made of walnut.

The base has a display that shows the current temperature and the selected temperature. The base also has a built-in timer that the user can start when they begin pouring.

## Specifications

- Capacity: 0.9 liters
- Power: 1,200 watts
- Temperature range: 40–100 °C
- Hold time: up to 60 minutes
- Materials: stainless steel body, walnut handle and knob
- Colors: black, white
- Warranty: two years

## Care

Descale the kettle once a month if you have hard water. Do not put the kettle or the base in the dishwasher. Wipe the outside with a soft, damp cloth. Oil the walnut handle with food-safe oil a few times a year to keep it from drying out.

# My First Garden

Last year I decided to start a vegetable garden. I had never grown vegetables before. I thought it would be a good way to spend more time outside and eat healthier food.

I chose a spot in the backyard that got sun in the afternoon. I dug up the grass and added compost to the soil. It took two weekends. My back was sore afterward.

In May I planted tomatoes, beans, zucchini and lettuce. I bought the tomato plants at a nursery and grew the others from seed. I watered the garden every evening after work.

The lettuce grew quickly. We ate salads from the garden for most of June. The beans also did well. The zucchini produced more than we could eat, so I gave some to the neighbors.

The tomatoes were a problem. The plants grew tall, but the fruit stayed green for a long time. In August some of the tomatoes split after a heavy rain. A few of the plants got a disease on their leaves. I ended up with fewer tomatoes than I expected.

I learned a lot from the experience. I learned that some vegetables are easier to grow than others. I learned that it is important to plan where to put each plant. I also learned that gardening takes more time than I thought it would.

This year I want to try again. I am going to plant fewer tomatoes and more beans. I also want to add herbs. In this essay I will describe what I learned in my first year and what I plan to do differently.

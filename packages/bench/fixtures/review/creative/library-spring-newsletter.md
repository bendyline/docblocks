# Spring Programs at the Oakmere Branch Library

The Oakmere Branch Library will offer several programs this spring. The programs are free and open to everyone. Registration is required for some of them.

## Book Club

The book club will meet on the second Tuesday of each month at 7 p.m. The book club will read three novels this spring. Copies of the novels are available to borrow at the front desk. New members are welcome, and you do not need to have finished the book to attend.

## Story Time

Story time for children ages three to five will take place on Wednesday mornings at 10 a.m. A librarian will read picture books. There will also be songs and a simple craft. Parents and caregivers should stay with their children during story time. Registration is required because space is limited to fifteen children.

## Seed Library

The library has started a seed library. Patrons can borrow seeds for vegetables, herbs and flowers. Patrons are asked to return some seeds at the end of the growing season if they can. The seed library is located near the magazine shelves. A volunteer from the Oakmere Garden Club will be at the seed library on the first Saturday of each month to answer questions.

## Computer Help

Volunteers will help patrons with computer questions on Saturday mornings from 9 a.m. to noon. Patrons can get help with email, online forms, printing and other basic tasks. Appointments are not required. Patrons may bring their own laptops or use the library's computers.

## Building Hours

The library is open Monday through Thursday from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Friday and Saturday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. It is closed on Sundays.

We hope to see you at the library this spring. For more information, please call the library or ask at the front desk.

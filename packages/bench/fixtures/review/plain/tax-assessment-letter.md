**Pellham County Office of the Assessor**

**Parcel number:** 04-221-0193

Dear Property Owner,

The Office of the Assessor reviews the value of every home in Pellham County every four years, as state law requires. We finished the latest review in March 2025, and the results for the subject property are set forth herein.

## How we reached the value

Home values in Owensford rose steadily over the last four years, driven mainly by new construction along the Ridley Creek corridor and by strong demand for homes near the new light-rail station, and our appraisers compared recent sales of similar homes within half a mile of your property to reach the new value.

The new AV takes effect for the 2026 tax year. Your tax bill will also depend on the tax rate that the county board sets each year.

## If you disagree

The property owner may request an informal review by calling the office within 30 days of the date of this notice. An informal review settles most questions.

If the informal review does not settle the matter, you shall file a formal appeal with the Board of Equalization within 45 days of the date of this notice. You can file online or in person, and there is no fee.

## Exemptions

Exemptions for seniors, veterans and people with disabilities may be claimed on Form PX-2, which can be obtained at our office or downloaded from our website. An exemption can lower your taxable value.

## Other information

Our office is open from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday to Friday. Please note that, as a result of this review, the assessed value of your home has gone up from $312,000 to $368,000.

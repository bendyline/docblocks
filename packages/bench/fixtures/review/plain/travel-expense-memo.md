# New Travel Expense Procedure

**To:** All staff, Corvane Health Partners  
**From:** Finance Office  
**Date:** 2 June 2025

From 1 July, you will claim travel expenses in the Expensa app instead of on paper forms. This memo explains what changes and what you need to do.

## What changes

- You will photograph receipts with your phone instead of mailing them to Finance.
- Your claim will go to the PTA for approval before we pay it.
- Mileage reimbursement for the utilization of a personal vehicle will be effectuated at the rate of 70 cents per mile.

## What you need to do

You shall file each claim within 30 days after your trip ends. Claims for trips taken heretofore may still be filed on paper until 31 July.

Employees traveling overnight must book lodging through the company travel portal so that the negotiated rate applies.

## Receipts

You need a receipt for every expense over $25. Meal receipts must be itemized; a card slip is not enough, and we do not repay alcohol. For hotels, attach the folio that shows the nightly rate and taxes, not the booking confirmation. For taxis and ride-share trips, a screenshot of the in-app receipt is fine. You can claim parking and tolls under $25 without a receipt, but list the date and place. If you lose a receipt, fill in a missing-receipt statement and have your manager sign it.

## Cancelled trips

If your trip is cancelled after you have already paid for a flight or hotel, you should still submit a claim for any costs that the airline or hotel will not refund, and attach the cancellation notice together with a short note from your manager explaining why the trip was cancelled and confirming that it was business-related.

## Payment

Payments will be made by the Finance Office within ten business days of approval, by direct deposit to your salary account.

# Residential Parking Permits for Harlow Hill

## Background

Pursuant to Ordinance 2024-17, the Ashgrove City Council has created a residential parking zone in the Harlow Hill district.

Commuters to the Ashgrove Medical Campus have been taking a growing share of the street parking near homes in the district, and at three public meetings last year the council heard from many residents who described circling their blocks for twenty minutes or more each evening and asked the council to act.

The zone will be managed by the Department of Transportation and Mobility.

## Who can get a permit

To get a permit, you shall live at an address inside the zone boundary shown on the [zone map](https://ashgrove.example/parking/harlow-hill-map). Each household can get up to two permits. Applicants are advised that each permit is tied to a single vehicle and cannot be moved to another car.

## How to apply

To apply, complete the online form at ashgrove.example/parking. Upload proof that you live in the zone, such as a current lease or deed, a utility bill from the last 60 days, or a bank statement showing your Harlow Hill address. You will also need your vehicle registration, which must show the same address. The fee is $40 per permit, paid by card when you apply. We will email you a confirmation number. Your permit will arrive by mail within ten business days of approval, or up to twenty business days in February, when most renewals come in.

## Applying in person

If you prefer to apply at City Hall, bring one document from the approved POR list and your vehicle registration. The permit counter is open from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on weekdays.

## Parking rules

If you park in the zone on a weekday between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. without a permit, you can get a $35 ticket. Your visitors can park for up to two hours without a permit.

## Other information

Place your permit on the inside of the rear window, in the lower left corner. Most important of all: enforcement starts on 1 April. Apply by 1 March so your permit arrives in time. Without a permit, you cannot park on your own street on weekdays from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.

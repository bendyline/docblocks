# Introducing Shared Boards in Plover

We're excited to announce Shared Boards, the most requested feature in Plover's history.

For years, teams have told us that keeping track of work across projects is hard. Plover has always let you organise tasks into boards, but each board belonged to one person, and the only way to show a teammate your plan was a screenshot.

Our team spent a lot of time thinking about how teams really work, and we really wanted to make something that would really help.

With Shared Boards, everyone on a team can see, edit and comment on the same board in real time, and changes appear instantly for every member.

## How to get started

Open any board, click **Share**, and invite you're teammates by email. They will get a link that opens the board in their browser or in the Plover app. Shared Boards is available on the Team plan, and it's free for everyone during the beta.

## Pricing

After the beta ends, Shared Boards will be included in the Team plan at no extra cost. Starter plan users can can upgrade at any time from the billing page.

## What's next

We are working on permissions, so that board owners can choose who edits a board and who only views it. This will come later this year. It will make it easier for them to manage it.

We also want Plover to help you organize your week, so look out for a calendar view in the spring. As always, tell us what you think in the community forum.

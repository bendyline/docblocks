# Remote Work Policy

**To:** All staff at Brightwater Design
**From:** People Operations
**Effective:** 1 March

## Purpose

This policy explains when and how staff can work remotely. It applies to all full-time employees.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to three days per week. New hires must work in the office for there first 90 days, so that they can meet their team and learn our tools.

## Requesting remote days

Remote days should be agreed with you manager at least one week in advance. Requests are submitted through the HR portal or by email or by talking to your manager or in the weekly team meeting.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and, on request, a moniter. Employees are responsible for their own internet connection, which must be reliable.

## Expectations

Remote staff must be reachable during core hours. It is expected that cameras will be turned on in meetings, where possible, unless there are reasons not to.

Remote days are not a replacement for childcare, and staff should take sick leave rather than working remotely when they are unwell.

## Part-time staff

Part-time staff may also work remotely up to two days per week, on the same terms.

## Review

Managers will review each employee's remote arrangement at the end of their six-month probation, and then once a year.

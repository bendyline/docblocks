# Setting Up the Shared Office Printer

This guide explains how to connect your laptop to the shared printer on the third floor, which is called Heron. Once it is set up, you can print from any app.

## Before you start

You need your staff login and about ten minutes.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Printers & scanners**.
2. Select **Add a printer**. Your laptop will search the network, this can take up to a minute.
3. Choose **Heron-3F** from the list. If it doesn't appear, make sure your connected to the office Wi-Fi, not the guest network.
4. When asked, enter the printer PIN you were sent during onboarding.
5. Print a test page to confirm that everything works.
6. Install the driver package from the IT portal. Heron will not show up in the list until the driver is installed.

## Printing on both sides

To print double-sided, select **Two-sided** in the print dialog. Note that the duplex functionality of the device may be constrained in certain circumstances, such as when utilizing heavier paper stock.

## Troubleshooting

If a job gets stuck, it should be cancelled from the queue.

If Heron shows as offline, restart you're laptop and try again. Most offline problems go away after a restart.

If problems persist, call the help desk at extension 4410.

## Who to contact

For any printer problem, contact IT directly at extension 4401. They are available from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. on weekdays.

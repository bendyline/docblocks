# Book Club Notes: _The Salt Road_ by Imogen Thale

The Harborside Readers met on 6 May at Della Finch's house. Eleven members came. A vote was held by the group in March, and _The Salt Road_ was chosen by a wide margin.

## Summary

_The Salt Road_ follows Mara Quell, a fourteen-year-old girl who leaves her inland village after a drought ruins her family's farm. She takes her younger brother, Tobin, and sets out for the coast, where an uncle works in the salt pans. On the road they meet a peddler who offers them a ride, a soldier who steals their food, and a widow who hides them from a press gang. Tobin fell ill near the coast, and Mara carried him the last two miles. The uncle was dead when they arrived, so Mara took his place in the salt pans and raised Tobin herself.

## Discussion

Most of us admired the writing, the plot moved too slowly in the middle. Della praised the descriptions of the salt pans, the press gang and the coastal storms. Several members did not pay attention to the map at the front of the book and were confused about the route.

Reading the final chapter, the ending felt rushed to many of us. Mara spends three hundred pages walking and then settles her whole future in four.

The book deals with a number of important themes in various interesting ways.

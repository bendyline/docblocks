# What We Learned from the Spring Inventory Count

**To:** Warehouse supervisors, Ostrander Supply  
**From:** Hal Brevik, Operations Manager

Our annual count took place on Saturday, 12 April, at the Millbrook warehouse. Forty-two staff counted 9,800 bin locations in a single day. This memo records what went well, what went wrong, and what we will change next year.

## What went well

The count started at 6 a.m., most teams finished by noon. The new handheld scanners saved hours, and the pre-printed bin sheets kept teams from counting the same location twice. We counted pallets, cartons and loose stock in one pass for the first time.

## What went wrong

There were some issues with various aspects of the process in certain areas.

Bay 4 finished last. The team started late, and the scanners there lost signal, and so we sent two people from Bay 2, and they helped for an hour, and then the count in Bay 4 ended at 4 p.m.

Walking the aisles after the count, several mislabeled bins were found by the supervisors.

The night shift did not remember to lock the high-value cage on Friday, so we had to recount it on Saturday morning.

A decision was made by the managers to delay Monday's shipping until the variances were checked.

## What we will change

Next year we will:

- order four more scanners for the far bays
- training new staff a week before the count
- the count should start on a Sunday, when no trucks arrive

Thank you to everyone who gave up a Saturday. The final variance was 0.6%, the lowest in five years.

# Information Note for Participants

## Third session of the Working Group

### Dates and venue

1. The third session of the IWG will be held from 3 to 5 November 2025 at the headquarters of the Regional Commission for Inland Waterways in Vienna. The Intergovernmental Working Group on Inland Water Transport (IWG) was established in 2021 to harmonize technical standards for river vessels.

2. 15 Member States have confirmed their participation. Observers from several national labor ministries are also expected.

### Registration

3. Participants should register at the registration center on the ground floor of Building B, which opens at 8 a.m. on 3 November. Registration closes at 1:30 PM on the first day.

4. Please don't be late — nobody likes sitting around waiting for stragglers.

### Credentials

5. Each head of delegation should ensure that his credentials, signed by the Head of State or Government or the Minister for Foreign Affairs, reach the secretariat before he arrives. A representative who cannot attend should arrange for his alternate to collect his badge. Delegates are welcome to bring their wives to the reception on the first evening.

### Programme of work

6. The opening meeting will begin at 10 a.m. on 3 November. Afternoon meetings will run from 3 p.m. to 6 p.m. The session is expected to close at 12.30 p.m. on 5 November.

7. The secretariat thanks the Government of South Korea for hosting the pre-session workshop on 2 November.

### Costs

8. The estimated cost of the session is $1245000, covered by extrabudgetary resources. Participants are responsible for their own travel and accommodation.

### Contact

9. Questions may be addressed to the secretariat of the Working Group at iwg-secretariat@rciw.example.

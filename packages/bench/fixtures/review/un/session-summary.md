# Committee on Dryland Agriculture Concludes Twelfth Session

## Meeting summary

1. The Committee on Dryland Agriculture concluded its twelfth session on 19 September 2025 after five days of deliberations attended by representatives of 41 Member States.

2. Opening the session, the chairman of the Committee welcomed three new members.

3. He recalled that irrigation schemes built in the 20th century now serve fewer than half of the farms they were designed for.

4. The Committee reviewed the WSDP, which the secretariat reported had reached 1.2 million farming households. Delegates welcomed the progress of the Water Security and Drought Preparedness Programme (WSDP) in its first two years.

5. The secretariat reported that average yields in participating districts rose by 12 percent, while water use per hectare fell by 8 per cent.

6. The representative of Turkey described a national drought insurance scheme that now covers smallholders in four provinces.

7. The average drought index for the region stood at .85, the highest since records began.

8. Several speakers stressed that every farmer must be able to feed his family and his workers, even in a year of failed rains.

9. In a message to the Committee, the secretary-general called on Member States to double investment in water storage by 2030.

10. In what can only be called a stunning triumph, the Committee smashed every expectation and delivered a truly historic package of decisions that will change dryland farming forever.

11. The Committee adopted three decisions, on drought early warning, on soil restoration and on the financing of water storage. The thirteenth session will be held from 7 to 11 September 2026.

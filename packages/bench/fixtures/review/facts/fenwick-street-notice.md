# Notice: Lead Service Line Replacement on Fenwick Street

The Marrowbank Water Department will replace the lead service lines on Fenwick Street, between Orchard Road and the river, this spring. This notice explains why the work is needed, what it costs you, and what to do afterwards.

## Why the work is needed

Lead service lines were laid across Marrowbank between 1910 and 1955, according to the department's [pipe inventory](https://water.marrowbank.example/inventory). Testing in 2023 found lead above the federal action level in 9 of the 40 Fenwick Street homes sampled; the results are in the [2023 sampling report](https://water.marrowbank.example/reports/fenwick-2023).

Lead exposure always causes lasting learning problems in children. Nationally, about one home in five still gets its water through a lead line. Marrowbank also has the oldest water system in the state, which makes the work more urgent here than anywhere else.

> "No amount of lead in drinking water is safe for a child."

## Schedule

Work begins on 14 April and will take six weeks, finishing by 9 May. Crews will work on one block at a time, from 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. on weekdays. Each home's water will be shut off for about four hours on the day its line is replaced, and crews will leave a door hanger two days before.

## What it costs you

Nothing. The project's $2.4 million budget, approved by the city council in January ([meeting minutes](https://marrowbank.example/council/2025-01-minutes)), covers all 85 homes on the street, including the section of pipe on private property. The $3.1 million project is paid for by a state infrastructure grant, so water rates will not rise to cover it.

## After the work

Disturbing old pipes can release lead for a short time. Because boiling water removes lead, boil all water you drink or cook with for two weeks after your line is replaced. After that, run the cold tap for five minutes before first use each morning, and keep using the filter pitcher the department will deliver to every home.

## Questions

Call the Water Department at 555-0148 or email fenwick@water.marrowbank.example. Staff will also be on Fenwick Street every weekday morning while the work is under way.

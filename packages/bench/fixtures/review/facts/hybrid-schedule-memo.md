# Memo: Moving Operations to a Hybrid Schedule

**To:** Operations staff, Tallis & Rowe Logistics  
**From:** Dana Whitcombe, Director of Operations  
**Date:** 3 March 2025

## Summary

From 7 April 2025, the operations team will work in the office on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays, and from home on Mondays and Fridays. Our six-month pilot cut ticket resolution time by a third, and this memo explains the change and what happens next.

## What the pilot found

From September 2024 to February 2025, 48 volunteers from the dispatch and billing groups worked the same schedule. The full results are in the [pilot report on the intranet](https://intranet.tallisrowe.example/ops/hybrid-pilot-2025).

- Average ticket resolution time fell from 6.2 hours to 5.4 hours, based on the help desk's monthly exports.
- Of the 48 volunteers, 36 (60%) asked to keep the schedule after the pilot ended.
- Missed handoffs between shifts held steady at about four a week, according to the shift-log audit in the same report.

## Why we are making the change

Hybrid schedules are now the industry standard, and nearly every logistics firm in the region has adopted one. Remote workers are 23% more productive than office workers. Our own pilot proves that working from home always improves focus.

As one researcher put it, "the commute is the most expensive hour of the working day." Two fewer commutes a week gives that time back to you.

The change also builds on our record. Our Kessling depot was the first in the country to run a fully paperless dispatch desk, and the same habits should make hybrid work smooth here.

## Timeline

- 17 March: managers confirm each person's office days.
- 24 March: IT finishes issuing laptops and headsets to anyone who still needs them.
- 1 April: the new schedule starts for all operations staff.
- 30 September: we review the first six months and report back.

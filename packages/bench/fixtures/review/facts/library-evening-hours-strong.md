# Evening Hours at the Brackwater Public Library: First-Year Results

In September 2024 the Brackwater Public Library moved its weekday closing time from 6 p.m. to 9 p.m. at its two largest branches, Harbor Street and Elm Park. This note summarizes the first year of results for the library board, drawing on the library's own records and two outside sources.

## Visits and borrowing

According to the library's [door-counter data](https://library.brackwater.example/data/visits-2025), the two branches recorded 61,200 visits between 6 p.m. and 9 p.m. from September 2024 to August 2025, about 18% of their total visits for the year. Loans checked out during those hours came to 24,900, according to the [circulation report](https://library.brackwater.example/data/circulation-2025).

These figures cannot tell us how many evening visitors are new to the library. Some of them may simply have moved visits they would otherwise have made earlier in the day.

## Who uses the evening hours

A survey of 412 evening visitors, run by the library in March 2025, found that 247 (60%) said they worked during the library's previous opening hours ([survey summary](https://library.brackwater.example/data/evening-survey-2025)). Because the survey reached only people already in the building, it may overstate how much the change matters to the wider community.

## Costs

The longer hours cost $186,000 in the first year, mostly in staff wages, according to the [finance office's year-end statement](https://library.brackwater.example/finance/fy2025). That works out to about $3 per evening visit.

## How Brackwater compares

A 2023 review of 14 libraries in the region by the Northgate Library Council found that libraries open past 8 p.m. tended to report more weekday visits, though the authors cautioned that larger, busier libraries were also more likely to stay open late ([Northgate Library Council, 2023](https://northgatelibraries.example/reports/late-hours-2023)). The review does not show that late opening causes higher use.

Head librarian Corin Ashby told the board in June that "the evening hours have changed who we see at the desk," while noting that the library has no data yet on whether those visitors return ([board minutes, 12 June 2025](https://library.brackwater.example/board/2025-06-12)).

## Recommendation

The first-year figures suggest the evening hours are well used. Staff recommend keeping them for a second year and adding a question about first-time visits to the next survey, so the board can judge the change on firmer evidence.

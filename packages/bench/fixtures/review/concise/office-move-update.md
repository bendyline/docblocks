# Update on the Office Move

**To:** All staff, Brindle & Park Architects  
**From:** Owen Castellan, Office Manager

I just wanted to take a quick moment to reach out to all of you and share a few thoughts about where things currently stand with the office move, since I know it has been on a lot of people's minds lately.

## Moving date

It has been decided by the partners that we will move into the new studio at 40 Gravel Lane on Friday, 13 June. In order to keep disruption to a minimum, the movers will arrive at 2 p.m. and work through the weekend. The studio opens as normal on Monday, 16 June.

## What you need to do

- Pack your desk into the crates that arrive on 9 June, and label each crate with your name and new desk number.
- Back up any files on your local drive to the shared server by Thursday.
- Take home any personal items you want to keep, such as plants and photos.

Please make sure that each and every crate is labeled, because unlabeled crates will go to the storage room.

## The new studio

The new studio is really very bright, with windows on three sides and a model shop twice the size of our current one. The ground floor has two meeting rooms, and the kitchen is upstairs. Bikes go in the courtyard.

Due to the fact that the lease on our current building ends on 30 June, we cannot push the date back. We will make a final decision on the seating plan once the partners have given consideration to the feedback from the survey.

## Parking

Gravel Lane's eight parking spaces are reserved for visiting clients. The nearest public car park is on Wren Street, a five-minute walk away.

## A reminder

To sum up and remind everyone once again: we are moving on Friday, 13 June, the movers arrive at 2 p.m., and the new studio opens on Monday, 16 June. Please pack and label your crates, back up your files, and take your personal things home before the move.

Send any questions to me.

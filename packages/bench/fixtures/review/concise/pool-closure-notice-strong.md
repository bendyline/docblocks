# Westbrook Pool Closes for Resurfacing, 2–27 June

Westbrook Community Pool will close from 2 June to 27 June while contractors replace the cracked tile lining of the main pool. The children's pool and changing rooms will close too, because the crew will store equipment on the deck.

## Why now

Inspectors found loose tiles near the deep end in March. A temporary repair has held, but the lining is 31 years old and cannot be patched again. June is our quietest month before the summer season.

## Swimming elsewhere

Your membership works at Hollins Leisure Centre during the closure at no extra cost. Show your membership card at the front desk. Hollins is open from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. on weekdays and from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. at weekends.

## Lessons and clubs

- **Swim lessons** move to Hollins on the same days and at the same times. Instructors will email parents the lane numbers.
- **Masters swimming** pauses for four weeks. June fees will be refunded automatically.
- **Aqua fitness** moves to the Hollins teaching pool on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10 a.m.

## Pausing your membership

If you would rather not swim at Hollins, we can freeze your membership for June and extend it by a month. Ask at reception or email members@westbrookpool.example by 30 May.

## Reopening

The pool reopens on Saturday, 28 June, with a free family swim from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. Thank you for your patience while we make the pool safe for the next thirty years.

# Customer Support Report: Third Quarter

## Overview

This report covers customer support at Fennimore Outdoor from July to September. Ticket volume rose, response times held steady, and the new help center cut password-reset requests.

## Ticket volume

We received 14,200 tickets this quarter, up from 12,900 last quarter. Most of the increase came from the Ridgeline tent launch, which generated a large number of questions about pole assembly.

## Response times

Median first response time was 3.1 hours, the same as last quarter. Weekend response times were slower, at 7.5 hours, because only two agents work on Saturdays and Sundays.

## The help center

The new help center went live on 8 August. Password-reset tickets fell by 40% in the following six weeks. It is our belief that the step-by-step reset article, now the most-viewed page on the site, is the main reason. The end result was 610 fewer password-reset tickets than in the second quarter.

## Customer satisfaction

Satisfaction was measured by a short survey that was sent after each ticket was closed, and the average score was found to be 4.3 out of 5.

## Recommendations

At the end of the day, what we really need to do going forward is basically make sure that weekend coverage gets improved.

Next quarter we plan to:

- add one agent to the weekend schedule
- publish a help-center article on assembling the Ridgeline tent poles
- conduct an analysis of whether chat support would shorten weekend response times

## Summary

In summary, to go over the main points of this report once more: ticket volume went up because of the Ridgeline launch, response times stayed the same except at weekends, the help center reduced password-reset tickets, and customers rated us 4.3 out of 5.

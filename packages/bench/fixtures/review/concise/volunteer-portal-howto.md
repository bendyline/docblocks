# How to Set Up Your Volunteer Portal Account

Basically, this guide is for new volunteers at the Larkspur Food Bank who are setting up a portal account for the first time. Setup takes about ten minutes.

## Before you start

You will need:

- the invitation email we sent you
- a phone that can receive text messages
- your availability for the next month

## Step 1: Accept the invitation

Open the invitation email and click **Join Larkspur**. The link expires after seven days; if yours has expired, ask the volunteer desk for a new one.

## Step 2: Create a password

Choose a password of at least 12 characters. At this point in time, the portal does not accept passwords that contain your name or email address.

## Step 3: Turn on text alerts

Enter your mobile number, then type in the six-digit code the portal sends you. Text alerts are absolutely essential for volunteers. Shift changes are sent by text message and are not sent by email.

## Step 4: Add your availability

Open the **Availability** tab and click each time slot when you are free and available. The scheduling team carries out a review of everyone's availability every Monday and makes an assignment of shifts by Wednesday.

## Step 5: Sign the volunteer agreement

The agreement is required to be read and signed by all volunteers before their first shift. It covers food safety, safe lifting, and our code of conduct.

## Troubleshooting

If the code does not arrive, wait two minutes and tap **Resend**. If you still cannot sign in, email volunteers@larkspurfood.example.

## Keep your account to yourself

Note that it is important to remember that your account is personal. Do not share your password with anyone, and do not let anyone else sign in for you, because your account is personal to you and it is the record of your volunteer hours.

# Writing Style Guide

This guide keeps our help articles consistent. Follow it for every article you write or edit.

## Voice

- Write to the reader as "you".
- Use short sentences and everyday words.
- Say what to do before explaining why.

## Numbers and dates

- Spell out numbers from one to nine, and use figures for 10 and above.
- Use figures for all percentages, and write "percent" as the % sign.
- Write times as 9:30 a.m., not 9.30am or 09:30.

## Formatting

- Use sentence case for headings.
- Use bold only for the names of buttons and menu items.
- Put each step of a procedure in its own numbered item.

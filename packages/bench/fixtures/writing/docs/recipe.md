# Roasted Tomato Soup

A simple soup for late summer, when tomatoes are cheap and ripe. It serves four.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
- 1 onion, quartered
- 4 cloves garlic, unpeeled
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
2. Spread the tomatoes, onion and garlic on a tray, drizzle with the oil, and season.
3. Roast for 40 minutes, until the tomatoes are soft and starting to char.
4. Squeeze the garlic out of its skins, then blend everything with the stock until smooth.
5. Warm the soup through, taste, and adjust the seasoning.

# New Starter FAQ

Welcome to Kestrel Analytics. These are the questions new starters ask most in their first week.

## When should I arrive on my first day?

Please arrive at 9:30 a.m. Your manager will meet you at reception and walk you to your desk.

## When will I get my laptop?

IT prepares your laptop before you start. You will collect it from the IT desk on the second floor on your first morning, and IT will help you sign in.

## How do I book time off?

Request time off in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead. Your manager approves the request, and you will get an email when it is approved.

## Who do I ask if I am stuck?

Every new starter has a buddy from another team for their first month. Your buddy's name is in your welcome email.

# Trip Report: Northwind Design Summit 2026

**Author:** Lena Ortiz, Product Design
**Dates:** 14–16 April 2026, Rotterdam

## Why I went

Our team is redesigning the Fieldnote mobile app this year, and the summit had three days of sessions on mobile design systems, research methods and accessibility. My goal was to bring back practices we could use in the redesign.

## Sessions I attended

On the first day, the keynote by the design lead of a large bank argued that design systems fail when only designers own them. Her team gave each component a named engineering owner and saw adoption across their apps rise from 40% to 85% in a year.

The research methods track on the second morning covered unmoderated usability tests. The speakers ran tests with 12 participants in two days, where moderated sessions had taken them two weeks.

On the third day I joined a workshop on offline-first apps. Fieldnote users often work in places with no signal, and the workshop showed how to tell people clearly which changes are saved only on the device.

## What it cost

The ticket was €640 and travel and two nights in a hotel came to €410, for a total of €1,050, within the €1,200 the team budgeted.

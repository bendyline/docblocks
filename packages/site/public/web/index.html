What the web app does

The web app is the same editor as the desktop app. Your documents stay on your device, and DocBlocks can see a folder only after you choose it.

Start right away

Create a document at docblocks.com. Write visually or in Markdown, preview it, add pictures, and export, all without an account.

Keep ordinary files

Keep documents in this browser or, in supported browsers, open a folder on your computer. Either way they are plain Markdown files.

Install it as an app

Open the app menu from the DocBlocks logo and choose Install DocBlocks… when your browser offers it. You can then start it from your desktop, dock, or taskbar. It uses the same browser profile and documents. The separate desktop app opens files directly in your own folders.

Limits of a browser

A browser keeps websites away from the rest of your computer. That limits what the web app can do, so keep copies of important work.

Browser storage is not a backup

A browser can delete a site’s data when storage runs low or when you clear its profile. Choose Protect data from browser cleanup in Settings when your browser offers it, and download backups regularly.

Folder support varies

Opening a folder on your computer depends on the browser and needs your permission. You may have to give permission again after a restart.

No access to the rest of your computer

The web app cannot use your operating system, a shell, or Git repositories. Use the desktop app when you need those.

Other DocBlocks apps

The desktop app adds native folders, menus, and optional AI and speech tools, and there are apps for iPhone, iPad, and Android. To move documents, export them as Markdown with media or as a DBK bundle. Workspaces do not sync automatically.

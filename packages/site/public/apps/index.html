Get DocBlocks

DocBlocks is on the App Store for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, on Google Play, and on the Microsoft Store. It is also a free download for Mac, Windows, and Linux, and it runs in any browser. Every version opens the same plain Markdown files, and none of them needs an account.

What it costs

  • Free in a browser, on Mac, Windows, and Linux, in VS Code, and on the command line
  • $9.99 once on the App Store, for iPhone, iPad, and Mac together
  • $9.99 once on Google Play and on the Microsoft Store
  • No subscription or in-app purchases
  • No account, ads, or tracking

iPhone, iPad, and Android

Each app costs $9.99, paid once. That is the US price; your store shows yours. You can write visually or in Markdown source, present a document as a slideshow, and export to PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), EPUB, and web pages.

iPhone and iPad

$9.99 once

Documents live in iCloud Drive › DocBlocks, or on the device when you are not signed in to iCloud. Either way they appear in the Files app as plain Markdown.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Android

$9.99 once

Keep documents in a folder you choose, where your other apps can open them too. Share and export through Android’s own controls.

Requires Android 9 or later.

Mac, Windows, and Linux

The desktop app is free. It opens folders on your computer and saves ordinary Markdown files there. It also has native menus and video export, and optional tools that run on your computer: an AI review of your draft, writing help, dictation, and read aloud.

Supporter Editions are $9.99 on the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store. Each is the same app as the free download, built from the same source. Your store keeps it up to date, and buying it supports DocBlocks. On the Mac, the same purchase also covers iPhone and iPad. Dictation, read aloud, and Git are only in the free Mac download, because App Store apps run in Apple’s sandbox.
